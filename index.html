Kerangka TWKA & CFRR

TWKA (Tema Wawasan Kristen Alkitabiah) diintegrasikan dengan kerangka CFRR di seluruh pembelajaran.

Creation — Penciptaan
Tuhan menciptakan segala sesuatu dengan baik dan teratur
Fall — Kejatuhan
Dosa merusak hubungan manusia dengan Tuhan dan sesama
Redemption — Penebusan
Yesus memulihkan dan mengajarkan jalan yang benar
Renewal — Pembaruan
Anak bertumbuh mencerminkan karakter Kristus

Alur Narasi Pembelajaran

"Aku mengenal diriku → Aku merespon dengan kreativitas → Aku mengenal dunia Tuhan → Aku melayani dengan tindakan"

Tema 1
Identitas dalam Kristus
Aku mengenal diriku
→
Tema 2
Ekspresi Kreativitas Beriman
Aku merespon dengan kreativitas
→
Tema 3
Mengenal Dunia Milik Allah
Aku mengenal dunia Tuhan
→
Tema 4
Pelayanan Terbaik di Dunia Milik Allah
Aku melayani dengan tindakan

Breakdown Capaian Pembelajaran — KB

Kelompok Bermain · Dengan TP, ATP dan CFRR · Fase Fondasi

CP: Anak mengenali, mengekspresikan dan mengelola emosi. Anak memiliki perilaku positif. Anak menggunakan fungsi gerak motorik.

CP: Anak mengenali informasi, mengkomunikasikan pikiran secara lisan dan tulisan. Anak menunjukkan minat pra membaca dan pra menulis.

CP: Anak mengenali konsep pra matematika untuk memecahkan masalah sehari-hari.

CP: Anak menunjukkan rasa ingin tahu melalui observasi, eksplorasi dan eksperimen.

CP: Anak menggunakan teknologi secara aman dan bertanggung jawab.

CP: Anak mengeksplorasi proses rekayasa sederhana.

CP: Anak mengeksplorasi proses seni, mengekspresikan dan mengapresiasi karya.

CP: Anak mengekspresikan diri melalui media seni dan mengembangkan imajinasi.

Breakdown Capaian Pembelajaran — TK A

Taman Kanak-kanak A · Dengan TP, ATP dan CFRR · Fase Fondasi

Klik judul setiap bidang pengembangan untuk membuka/menutup detail capaian.

CP: Anak percaya kepada Tuhan YME, memahami dan mempraktikkan ajaran pokok. Anak menjaga kebersihan, kesehatan dan keselamatan diri. Anak menghargai sesama dengan perbedaannya.

CP: Anak mengenali, mengekspresikan dan mengelola emosi serta membangun hubungan sosial. Anak memiliki perilaku positif terhadap diri dan lingkungan. Anak menggunakan fungsi gerak untuk eksplorasi.

CP: Anak mengenali informasi, mengkomunikasikan pikiran lisan dan tulisan. Anak menunjukkan minat dan berpartisipasi dalam kegiatan pra membaca dan pra menulis.

CP: Anak mengenali dan menggunakan konsep pra matematika untuk memecahkan masalah dalam kehidupan sehari-hari.

CP: Anak menunjukkan kemampuan dasar berpikir kritis, kreatif dan kolaboratif. Anak menunjukkan rasa ingin tahu melalui observasi, eksplorasi dan eksperimen.

CP: Anak mengenal dan menggunakan teknologi serta alat secara aman dan bertanggung jawab.

CP: Anak mengeksplorasi proses rekayasa sederhana dan memecahkan masalah dengan kreatif.

CP: Anak mengeksplorasi proses seni, mengekspresikan dan mengapresiasi berbagai karya seni.

CP: Anak mengekspresikan diri melalui media seni dan mengembangkan imajinasi dengan berbagai cara.

Breakdown Capaian Pembelajaran — TK B

Taman Kanak-kanak B · Dengan TP, ATP dan CFRR · Fase Fondasi

Klik judul setiap bidang pengembangan untuk membuka/menutup detail capaian.

CP: Anak percaya kepada Tuhan YME, memahami dan mempraktikkan ajaran pokok secara mandiri. Anak menjaga kebersihan, kesehatan dan keselamatan. Anak menghargai sesama dan berakhlak mulia.

CP: Anak mengelola emosi dengan baik dan membangun hubungan sosial yang sehat. Anak memiliki kepercayaan diri dan perilaku positif. Anak menggunakan fungsi gerak untuk eksplorasi dan manipulasi lingkungan.

CP: Anak mengenali informasi dan mengkomunikasikan pikiran secara lisan dan tulisan. Anak berpartisipasi aktif dalam membaca dan menulis awal.

CP: Anak mengenal dan menggunakan konsep matematika awal untuk memecahkan masalah sehari-hari secara mandiri.

CP: Anak menunjukkan kemampuan berpikir kritis dan kreatif, serta menyelidiki dunia melalui observasi dan eksperimen yang lebih kompleks.

CP: Anak menggunakan teknologi dan alat secara mandiri, aman, dan bertanggung jawab.

CP: Anak merencanakan, membangun dan mengevaluasi proses rekayasa secara lebih mandiri dan sistematis.

CP: Anak mengeksplorasi dan mengekspresikan diri melalui berbagai proses seni dengan lebih mandiri dan penuh penghayatan.

CP: Anak mengekspresikan ide dan imajinasi melalui berbagai media secara mandiri dan percaya diri.

English Scope & Sequence — KB

Kelompok Bermain · TP 2026-2027

CP: Anak mengenali dan memahami berbagai informasi, mengkomunikasikan pikiran dan perasaan secara lisan dan tulisan. Anak menunjukkan minat dalam kegiatan pra membaca dan pra menulis.
Semester 1
E1.1 Listening and responding to simple instructions in English (body parts and family)
Activities: "Touch your nose", "Show me your hands"; Singing "Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes"
Creation
God created our ears to hear and understand language — even new languages
Fall
Sometimes we don't listen or get frustrated when we don't understand
Redemption
Jesus listened to everyone and spoke so all could understand
Renewal
Children enjoy listening to English words and respond with enthusiasm
E1.2 Listening to and enjoying English songs and stories about self and family
Activities: "I Love You" song, "Family Finger Song"; Picture books about families
Creation
God created music and stories in every language to bring joy
Fall
Unfamiliarity can make us resist new songs and stories
Redemption
God's love is expressed in every language and culture
Renewal
Children enjoy English songs and stories about themselves and family
E1.3 Imitating English sounds and words (body parts, feelings, family)
Vocabulary: head, eyes, nose, mouth, hand, foot; happy, sad, angry; mommy, daddy, brother, sister
Creation
God gave humans the gift of language — every language reflects His creativity
Fall
Fear of making mistakes can stop us from trying new words
Redemption
God is patient with us as we learn — He celebrates every effort
Renewal
Children confidently repeat English words about body, feelings and family
E1.4 Showing positive behavior toward English picture books
Activities: Holding English books, pointing at pictures, naming in English
Creation
God gave us books and pictures to learn about His world in many languages
Fall
Laziness or disinterest can keep us from discovering new things
Redemption
God's Word is a lamp — learning to read opens doors (Psalm 119:105)
Renewal
Children show interest and excitement when looking at English books
E1.5 Making marks and simple writing related to self
Activities: Tracing letters of their name; Drawing self-portrait and labeling (eyes, nose, mouth)
Creation
God gave us hands to create and express ourselves through writing
Fall
Fear of imperfection can stop us from trying to write
Redemption
God values every effort — not perfection
Renewal
Children bravely attempt to trace English letters
E1.6 Telling about their drawing using simple English words
Activities: "This is me", "This is my family", "I am happy"; Show-and-tell
Creation
God gave us the ability to share our stories with others
Fall
Shyness can prevent us from speaking up
Redemption
Jesus used simple stories to teach — simple words carry big meaning
Renewal
Children proudly describe their drawings using basic English phrases
E2.1 Listening and responding to English praise songs
Activities: "Clap your hands", "Raise your hands"; "God is Good", "Jesus Loves Me"
Creation
God created praise as a way to express love — in every language
Fall
We can feel shy or unwilling to praise
Redemption
Psalm 150 calls us to praise God with everything we have
Renewal
Children joyfully respond to English praise songs with actions
E2.2 Listening to English stories about God's love
Activities: Simple Bible stories (Creation, Noah's Ark, Good Shepherd); Felt board stories
Creation
God's love story is told in every language
Fall
Language barriers can feel like obstacles
Redemption
God's message of love transcends all languages
Renewal
Children enjoy hearing about God's love in English
E2.3 Imitating English words related to creativity and praise
Vocabulary: sing, dance, clap, pray, thank you, love, beautiful, color, paint, draw, happy, God, Jesus
Creation
God fills our mouths with words of praise and creativity
Fall
Hesitation to try new words can limit expression
Redemption
God gives us words to praise Him — even in a new language
Renewal
Children enthusiastically use English praise and art words
E2.4 Showing interest in English books about art
Activities: Books about colors, shapes, art; Naming art materials (crayon, paper, paint)
Creation
God is the master Artist — He inspires creativity in every language
Fall
Disengagement can limit creativity
Redemption
God opens our eyes to see beauty and inspires us to create
Renewal
Children explore English art books and name materials
E2.5 Making creative marks with English labels
Activities: Drawing and writing color names; Labeling art projects (red, blue, circle, star)
Creation
God gave us the ability to combine art and language
Fall
Perfectionism can steal the joy of creative expression
Redemption
God sees beauty in every effort
Renewal
Children create art and label it with English words
E2.6 Telling about art using simple English praise expressions
Activities: "I made this for God", "This is beautiful", "Thank you God"
Creation
God gave us the gift of expressing gratitude through words and art
Fall
We sometimes forget to say thank you
Redemption
A thankful heart speaks in every language
Renewal
Children share creations using simple English expressions of gratitude
Semester 2
E3.1 Listening and responding to simple instructions about nature
Activities: "Find something green", "Show me the flower", "Where is the cat?"; Animal action songs
Creation
God created a world full of wonderful things to discover and name
Fall
We often take the beauty of nature for granted
Redemption
Jesus taught us to look at flowers and birds (Matthew 6:26-28)
Renewal
Children respond to English instructions about nature with excitement
E3.2 Listening to and enjoying English songs about animals and nature
Activities: "Old MacDonald Had a Farm", "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star"; Animal sound stories

Fall
We sometimes don't appreciate the wonder of God's creatures

Children love singing English songs about God's creation
E3.3 Imitating English words (animals, plants, colors, nature)
Vocabulary: cat, dog, fish, bird, tree, flower, sun, moon, star, water, rain; red, blue, green, yellow
Creation

Fall
Limited vocabulary shouldn't stop us from expressing ourselves
Redemption
God patiently teaches us — one word at a time
Renewal
Children eagerly learn English names for animals, plants and colors
E3.4 Showing positive behavior toward English nature books
Activities: Looking at nature picture books; Pointing and naming in English
Creation
God reveals His creation through pictures and words in every language
Fall
Disinterest can keep us from discovering beauty in books
Redemption
God opens our eyes to see wonder in His creation
Renewal
Children enjoy exploring English books about animals and nature
E3.5 Making drawings of nature with English labels
Activities: Drawing animals and writing first letter (C for cat); Coloring nature scenes
Creation
God gave us creativity to represent His creation through art and words
Fall
Frustration with writing can discourage us
Redemption
God celebrates our creative efforts — every mark matters
Renewal
Children draw nature scenes and attempt English labels
E3.6 Telling about nature drawings using simple English
Activities: "This is a cat", "The flower is red", "I see a star"
Creation
God gave us language to describe and appreciate His world
Fall
Shyness or limited vocabulary can hold us back
Redemption
Even simple words can express big wonder — "God is good!"
Renewal
Children describe their nature drawings using simple English
E4.1 Listening and responding to English hygiene instructions
Activities: "Wash your hands", "Brush your teeth", "Clean up time"
Creation
God designed our bodies to be cared for — cleanliness honors Him
Fall
Laziness can make us neglect caring for ourselves
Redemption
God teaches us our body is His temple (1 Corinthians 6:19)
Renewal
Children follow English hygiene instructions with understanding
E4.2 Listening to English stories about sharing and kindness
Activities: Stories about sharing; "If You're Kind and You Know It"
Creation
God created us to share and care for one another
Fall
Selfishness makes us want to keep everything
Redemption
Jesus said it is more blessed to give than receive (Acts 20:35)
Renewal
Children enjoy English stories about kindness
E4.3 Imitating English words for manners and caring
Vocabulary: please, thank you, sorry, excuse me, share, help, clean, wash, friend, kind
Creation
God gave us polite words to build good relationships
Fall
Rudeness and forgetfulness can hurt relationships
Redemption
Jesus modeled perfect manners — kindness, patience, grace
Renewal
Children use English polite words naturally
E4.4 Showing interest in English books about helping
Activities: Picture books about helpers, community, kindness; Naming helpers (teacher, doctor, farmer)
Creation
God gave people different roles to serve and help
Fall
We can be too focused on ourselves
Redemption
Jesus came to serve, not to be served (Mark 10:45)
Renewal
Children enjoy English books about helpers
E4.5 Making drawings about helping with English labels
Activities: Drawing pictures of helping; Writing/tracing "help", "share", "friend"
Creation
God gave us hands to help and create
Fall
Laziness can stop us from helping
Redemption
God empowers us to do good works (Ephesians 2:10)
Renewal
Children draw acts of kindness and label in English
E4.6 Telling about kindness using simple English
Activities: "I help my friend", "I share my toys", "Please and thank you"
Creation
God gave us words to encourage and appreciate
Fall
We sometimes forget to acknowledge kindness
Redemption
Kind words are like honey — sweet and healing (Proverbs 16:24)
Renewal
Children describe acts of kindness in simple English

English Scope & Sequence — TK A

Taman Kanak-kanak A · TP 2026-2027

CP: Anak mengenali dan memahami berbagai informasi, mengkomunikasikan pikiran dan perasaan secara lisan dan tulisan. Anak menunjukkan minat dalam kegiatan pra membaca dan pra menulis. Plus more advanced literacy skills.
Semester 1
E1.1 Speaking clearly using simple English sentences about self and family
Activities: "My name is...", "I am ___ years old", "I have ___ eyes"; Self-introduction
Creation
God gave each person a unique identity to share
Fall
Shyness can prevent us from sharing who we are
Redemption
God calls each of us by name (Isaiah 43:1)
Renewal
Children confidently introduce themselves in English
E1.2 Listening and following 2-3 step instructions in English
Activities: "Stand up, clap your hands, and sit down"; TPR games
Creation
God gave us ability to listen and follow instructions in sequence
Fall
Distraction makes us miss important steps
Redemption
God's instructions are always for our good
Renewal
Children follow multi-step English instructions confidently
E1.3 Recognizing and pointing to English letter symbols
Activities: Letter hunt games, magnetic letters, tracing letters of name; "Find the letter A"
Creation
God inspired written language so we can communicate
Fall
Impatience with learning letters can lead to frustration
Redemption
Every letter learned is a step toward reading God's Word
Renewal
Children enthusiastically recognize English letters
E1.4 Identifying English words that start with the same letter
Activities: "Ball, book, bird — they all start with B!"; Sorting picture cards
Creation
God created patterns in language that help us learn
Fall
Difficulty seeing patterns can feel overwhelming
Redemption
God gives wisdom to those who seek it (James 1:5)
Renewal
Children recognize initial letter patterns in English
E1.5 Recognizing first letter of their name and matching to sound
Activities: "My name starts with 'S' — sss like snake!"; Phonics introduction
Creation
God gave each child a special name with letters and sounds
Fall
We might feel our name is ordinary
Redemption
God calls us by name — every name is precious (Isaiah 43:1)
Renewal
Children proudly identify letters and sounds in their name
E1.6 Drawing and telling a story in simple English
Activities: Drawing "My Family" and describing: "This is my daddy, he is tall"; Show-and-tell
Creation
God gave us creativity to express stories through pictures and words
Fall
Fear of speaking incorrectly can silence us
Redemption
Jesus used simple stories — our stories matter too
Renewal
Children confidently describe drawings using English sentences
E2.1 Speaking clearly about creative and worship activities in English
Activities: "I like to sing for God", "This painting is for Jesus", "Let's praise God together"
Creation
God designed worship as creative expression — art, music, dance, words
Fall
Self-consciousness can block creative worship
Redemption
Psalm 150 — Praise Him with everything that has breath!
Renewal
Children express worship through English words with joy
E2.2 Listening and following instructions for Bible drama activities
Activities: "You will be David, stand here and pretend to play the harp"; Bible story acting
Creation
God inspired dramatic stories in the Bible for us to learn from
Fall
Fear of performing can hold us back
Redemption
God gives courage to those who step out in faith
Renewal
Children follow English drama instructions and participate
E2.3 Recognizing English letters in worship and art vocabulary
Activities: S-I-N-G, P-R-A-Y, L-O-V-E, G-O-D; Letter recognition through worship word cards
Creation
God's name and praise words are written in every language
Fall
Learning to read worship words can feel challenging
Redemption
Reading God's name is the beginning of knowing Him
Renewal
Children recognize key worship words in English
E2.4 Identifying creative words that share initial sounds
Activities: "Paint, praise, pray — /p/ sound!"; "Sing, song, star — /s/ sound!"
Creation
God created language patterns that enhance expression
Fall
Confusion with sounds can slow progress
Redemption
God is patient — every sound learned brings us closer to reading
Renewal
Children identify beginning sounds in creative vocabulary
E2.5 Writing letters of simple English worship/art words
Activities: Tracing G-O-D, L-O-V-E; Writing initial letters of art supplies
Creation
God inspired people to write His words
Fall
Writing difficulties shouldn't discourage us
Redemption
God values every letter written in His name
Renewal
Children write simple English worship and art words
E2.6 Drawing creative projects and describing in English
Activities: "I made a card for my friend — it says LOVE"; Art exhibition with English descriptions
Creation
God gave us creativity and language to bless others
Fall
We sometimes keep our gifts instead of sharing
Redemption
Creating for others reflects God's generous nature
Renewal
Children describe creative projects in English and share them
Semester 2
E3.1 Speaking clearly about animals, plants and weather in English

Proposal Perubahan TWKA

Dari 6 Tema menjadi 4 Tema per Tahun Pelajaran

Ringkasan Perubahan

Tahun Lalu — 6 Tema

  • 3 tema per semester
  • ±5–8 minggu per tema
  • 3 sub-tema per tema
  • Waktu per tema lebih singkat
Tema yang dihapus:
  • Kuasa Allah dalam Sejarah
  • Pengaturan Komunitas Orang Percaya

Tahun Ini — 4 Tema

  • 2 tema per semester
  • ±9 minggu per tema
  • 4 sub-tema per tema
  • Waktu lebih untuk pendalaman
Tema yang dipertahankan & diperluas:
  • Identitas dalam Kristus
  • Ekspresi Kreativitas Beriman
  • Mengenal Dunia Milik Allah
  • Pelayanan Terbaik di Dunia Milik Allah

Alasan Perubahan

Tema "Kuasa Allah dalam Sejarah" dihapus

Konsep sejarah terlalu abstrak untuk anak usia 3–6 tahun di fase fondasi. Lebih efektif mengintegrasikan narasi sejarah Alkitab secara tematis dalam cerita konkret.

Tema "Pengaturan Komunitas Orang Percaya" dihapus

Konsep komunitas gereja dan tata kelola organisasi orang percaya lebih sesuai untuk jenjang yang lebih tinggi. Pada fondasi, fokus lebih produktif pada identitas pribadi dan relasi langsung.

Manfaat Perubahan

+4 minggu
Waktu lebih per tema
(9 vs 5–8 minggu)
4 sub-tema
Per tema
(sebelumnya 3)
Lebih dalam
Eksplorasi konsep
lebih mendalam
Lebih sesuai
Perkembangan anak
usia fondasi

Alur Narasi Teologis yang Lebih Kohesif

Dengan 4 tema, alur narasi antar tema menjadi lebih jelas dan berkesinambungan:

Tema 1: Identitas dalam Kristus — Siapa aku?
↓ (Dari mengenal diri, beralih ke merespon)
Tema 2: Ekspresi Kreativitas Beriman — Bagaimana aku merespon?
↓ (Dari merespon, meluas ke mengenal dunia)
Tema 3: Mengenal Dunia Milik Allah — Di mana aku hidup?
↓ (Dari mengenal dunia, beralih ke tindakan nyata)
Tema 4: Pelayanan Terbaik di Dunia Milik Allah — Apa yang aku lakukan?
